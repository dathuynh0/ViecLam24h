TRANG THONG TIN UNG VIEN - NHA TUYEN DUNG

Da xay dung them 2 trang ReactJS:

1. Trang thong tin ung vien
   Duong dan chay: http://localhost:5173/#/candidate-profile
   File chinh: frontend/src/pages/CandidateProfilePage.jsx
   Noi dung:
   - Thong tin ca nhan ung vien
   - Muc do hoan thien ho so
   - Muc tieu nghe nghiep
   - Ky nang chuyen mon
   - CV, hoc van, kinh nghiem
   - Danh sach CV da nop va trang thai

2. Trang thong tin nha tuyen dung
   Duong dan chay: http://localhost:5173/#/company-profile
   Hoac: http://localhost:5173/#/employer-profile
   File chinh: frontend/src/pages/EmployerProfilePage.jsx
   Noi dung:
   - Ho so cong ty/nha tuyen dung
   - Thong tin doanh nghiep
   - Mo ta cong ty
   - Phuc loi
   - Tin tuyen dung dang mo
   - Lien he tuyen dung

Cac file da them/sua:
- frontend/src/App.jsx
- frontend/src/components/Header.jsx
- frontend/src/pages/CandidateProfilePage.jsx
- frontend/src/pages/EmployerProfilePage.jsx
- frontend/src/styles.css

Luu y:
- Khong can sua backend cho phan giao dien nay.
- Du lieu dang la du lieu mau tren frontend.
- Sau nay co API profile thi chi can thay phan du lieu mau bang fetch API.
